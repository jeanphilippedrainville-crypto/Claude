Welcome to the natty bar

Thirsty regulars roll in with very specific vibes. Read what they want, pour them the right low-intervention bottle before their patience runs dry, and stack up a combo. Three bad pours and the night's over.

Natural wine, fast: grapes farmed organically/biodynamically, fermented with wild yeast, nothing added, nothing taken away — little to no added sulphites. Often cloudy, funky, and gloriously alive. 🌱

How to pour

Each guest describes a mood, occasion, or pairing. Tap the bottle from your cellar that fits best. Match the colour swatch and the style to their ask. The faster you pour, the bigger the tip — and your combo multiplier grows with every correct pour in a row. Let the patience bar empty and you lose a heart.
