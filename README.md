# 🍷 Glou Glou — A Natural Wine Bar Game

A tiny, fast, single-file game for someone who loves natural wine.

You're running a hip natty bar. Regulars roll in with very specific vibes —
*"something orange and weird,"* *"a red I can chill and gulp,"* *"bubbles for my birthday"* —
and you pour the right low-intervention bottle before their patience runs out.
Stack a combo, chase a high score, and pick up a little wine knowledge along the way.

## How to play

1. Open **`index.html`** in any web browser (double-click it — no install, works offline).
2. Read what each guest wants, then tap the bottle that fits.
3. The faster you pour, the bigger the tip. A correct pour in a row grows your combo.
4. Three bad pours and it's last call. Beat your best score.

Tip: on a keyboard, press **1–8** to pour the matching bottle.

## What's in the cellar

Real natural-wine styles — Pét-Nat, skin-contact (orange), glou-glou reds,
carbonic Gamay, biodynamic whites, amphora/qvevri, piquette, and zero-sulphite
bottlings — each with a one-line note on what makes it special.
